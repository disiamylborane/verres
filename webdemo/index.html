Verres unit-aware calculator demo

This page is a playground for the syntax of verres type system.

Syntax

Values

  • Number: 42, 42.2 (rational), 42/5 (rational, no spaces), 42.2~ (imprecise real), 42.2~24 (imprecise real with precision of 24 bits)
  • Number with unit: 42 m, 42~ m2, 42 kg m2 s-1, 42 N kg-1

Expressions

  • Addition and subtraction: 4 m + 200 cm is 6 m, 4 m - 200 cm is 2 m
  • Multiplication and division: 4 m * 200 cm is 8 m2, 4 m / 200 cm is 2. Warning: a/b without spaces is a rational literal, 3 / 5 m = 0.6 m-1, but 3/5 m = 0.6 m
  • Accumulation operations: a +^ b = a * (1 + b), a -^ b = a * (1 - b), 5 m +^ 20 % is 6 m, 10 kg -^ 20 % is 8 kg
  • Exponential growth distribution: 256 // 2 min is 16 // min (growth by 256 times per minute, expressed as times per minute)
  • Exponential growth accumulation: 16 // min ** 30 s is 4 (growth by 16 times per minute, how much per half a minute)
  • Conversion: 4 m | cm is 400 cm
  • Conversion to base units: 4 N || is 4 m kg s-2
  • Brackets for grouping: ((16 // min ||) ** 30 s) * 2 is 8

Variables

  • Introduce: _x = 3 (Variables always have prefix _)
  • Use: _x + _x (Variables always have prefix _)
  • _ is <last answer>: 2 = 2, in the next statement _ = 2

Helpers

  • Type ? for a list of units
  • Type ? <unit name> for a detailed information on unit
